How it works

Heritage, taught the way children actually learn.

  • Live on WhatsApp

    Classes run on WhatsApp video, the app your family already uses. Nothing new to download.

  • 30 mins, twice a week

    Short, consistent sessions that fit around school and a busy family.

  • Songs & games

    Children absorb a language through rhythm and play, long before they notice they are learning.

  • Group or private

    Join a small group of peers, or choose one-to-one attention tailored to your child.
